OFFICIAL GDG EVENT BRIEF

Glitches and Giggles: Mission Directive

⚠️
THE GOLDEN RULE: Normal UI/UX prioritizes ease, accessibility, and speed. Here, your goal is to make a user laugh, groan, and question reality—while still crafting an ingenious, functional disaster!

🎯 The 4 Judging Dimensions (Peer Scored)

1

Comic Genius & Wit (30%)

How funny, witty, and clever is the concept? Does it parody annoying modern software trends?

2

Creative Frustration (30%)

Does it invent brand new ways to baffle the user? Unhinged sliders, evasive buttons, upside-down scroll!

3

Cohesion & Polish (20%)

Is the disaster well-crafted? A purposefully bad design looks vastly different from a lazy draft.

4

AI & Tool Mastery (20%)

Innovative prompting and creative exploitation of AI tools to generate absurd assets and copy.

⏱️ Challenge Schedule (90-Minute Breakdown)

  • 00:00 – 00:15: Opening briefing & topic reveal by GDG organizers.
  • 00:15 – 01:45 (90 Min): The Chaos Sprint! Ideate, build in Figma/Code/Canva, generate AI assets.
  • 01:45 – 02:15: Interactive Peer Showcase & Mutual Torment Testing.
  • 02:15 – 02:30: Peer Score Tally, Crowning of Champions & Goodies distribution.

🛠️ Permitted Tools & Guidelines

Figma Canva Pen & Paper HTML / CSS / JS Midjourney / Imagen ChatGPT / Gemini / Claude Photoshop PowerPoint / Slides

Any medium is permitted as long as peers can interact with or view your submission!
